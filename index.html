How to play
The goal is simple: jump higher and higher.

Land on platforms to bounce. Use double-jump when the gap is too big.

Every 500 points a new feature unlocks:
• Moving platforms
• Vanishing platforms
• Small / narrow platforms
• Springs (super jump)
• Wind
• Darkness

You can play yourself or let the neural network play and learn.
About the AI
The agent uses a small neural net (17 → 16 → 4) trained with REINFORCE + entropy bonus.

Reward = height gained. It learns purely in the browser and saves progress in localStorage.
